## Useful material and current disposition

- **SI prefixes:** The handout includes giga through pico, which is already reflected in the electronics-fundamentals draft. Its kilo symbol is printed as uppercase `K`; use lowercase `k` and check SI symbols against the BIPM table.
- **LED resistor challenge:** The stated 15 mA target and 2 V LED drop are already used as explicitly assumed example values in the resistor draft. Select the actual resistor using the real LED and supply data; do not treat the exercise assumptions as universal specifications.
- **Battery-sizing challenge:** The motor’s stated 6 V, 150 mA, and 48 h requirements and the exercise’s cell table are recorded in the battery draft. The handout identifies no cell models or test conditions, so its capacities and “typical drain” values are arithmetic practice only, not product specifications or runtime guarantees.
- **Parts list:** The PDF lists two 9 V batteries and connectors, a 6.15 V, 0.5 A miniature lamp and holder, a red LED, a 470 Ω resistor, and two DMM models. This matches the item summary in the electronics-fundamentals draft. The sheet supplies US-oriented shop links and does not show the circuit wiring. Its visual layout has been checked, but do not reproduce the screenshot or its product imagery without checking reuse rights.
- **DMM list (PDF removed):** The handout named SparkFun TOL-12966 and Extech MN36 and showed a meter photograph, but provided no specifications. The user reports having reviewed the handout and requested that the PDF not be retained. Check the current manufacturer manuals and UK suitability before recommending either model; this source summary is not a substitute for those checks.

## Disposition

The course’s useful teaching examples are already represented in the drafts; no further technical content needs to be copied into the Scout booklet or Leaders’ Guide. The four remaining handouts can be kept as provenance for the course examples. The DMM handout is not retained; the separate manual and UK-suitability check remains in [TODO.md](../TODO.md).
