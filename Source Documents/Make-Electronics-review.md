# Review note: *Make: Electronics*

- **Reviewed:** 2026-11-06
- **Author:** Charles Platt
- **Edition information in PDF:** First edition, December 2009
- **Length:** 351 PDF pages

## Review scope

I extracted the searchable text and compared the book with the Scouts booklet, Leaders’ Guide, and planned blog posts. I did not visually inspect the pages, photographs, or circuit diagrams. Text extraction and the book’s US context limit how much can be checked from this review. The book is copyrighted; no passages, diagrams, or photographs have been reproduced.

## Useful ideas for the project

The book’s experiment-based structure covers basic circuits, resistors and Ohm’s law, variable resistors, series and parallel circuits, switches, capacitors, transistors, logic, measurement, soldering, and prototype boards. Much of this overlaps with the learner booklet.

Its learning approach is useful for Leaders: invite a prediction, change one feature at a time, observe and measure the result, then explain it. A short guidance section on prediction and measurement has been added to the [Leaders’ Guide](../Leaders%20Guide.md). Apply this approach only to a circuit that has been checked for the actual parts and supply.

## Material not adopted

The book includes activities that are unsuitable for Scouts, including tasting the output of a 9 V battery, deliberately shorting or abusing batteries, and intentionally overheating or destroying LEDs and other components. Do not use these activities as demonstrations. Teach the underlying ideas with checked, non-destructive circuits and measurements.

Do not treat the book’s soldering and chemical-safety discussion as current UK guidance. It makes broad claims about modern solder and health risks, and does not provide the fume-control guidance now linked in the Leaders’ Guide. Follow current HSE guidance, product safety information, and Scouts’ activity risk-assessment process.

The book uses US suppliers, prices, wire gauges, and mains assumptions, and it dates from 2009. These details were not copied into project material. Check technical information against the specific component datasheet and current UK guidance before adapting it.

## Disposition

`Source Documents/Make - Electronics.pdf` was deleted from the repository after the review, following the established source-review workflow. This note records the review and its limits; it is not a substitute for the source document.
