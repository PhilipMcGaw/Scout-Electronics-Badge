====== Review note: Electronics.pdf ======

* **Reviewed:** 2026-11-06
* **Source filename:** ''Electronics.pdf''
* **Cover title:** //Electronics//, Scouting America Merit Badge Series
* **Length:** 89 PDF pages

===== Review scope =====

I reviewed the searchable text in the PDF and compared it with the booklet and blog outlines. Some pages contain image-only material and were not visually inspected, so this review does not assess the accuracy or reuse rights of the PDF’s diagrams and photographs.

===== Useful points carried into the project =====

* The schematic chapter recommends matching symbols and connection lines to the real components and wiring. This concept is already explained in the booklet; the Woggle KiCad blog outline now adds a practical exercise to follow a connection from the schematic to PCB pads.
* The careers chapter suggests that young people explore the kinds of work done in electronics. A short, optional career-exploration prompt has been added to the booklet’s “What to try next” section.
* Its discussion of choosing a complete kit or sourcing parts separately is already covered in the booklet’s “Buying Parts” section.
* Its basic component, soldering, and multimeter coverage overlaps with material already in the booklet. No new technical instructions were copied from it.

===== Material not adopted =====

The PDF includes US-specific references, dated suppliers and equipment, and older soldering instructions. These were not carried into the project. Check current product instructions and applicable safety guidance before using any similar procedure.

===== Disposition =====

''Source Documents/Electronics.pdf'' was deleted from the repository after the review, at Philip’s request. This note preserves the review scope and the decisions made; it is not a substitute for the source document.
