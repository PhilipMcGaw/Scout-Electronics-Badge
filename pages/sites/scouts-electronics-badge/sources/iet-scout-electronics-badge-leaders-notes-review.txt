====== Review note: IET Scout Electronics Badge Leaders’ Pack ======

* **Reviewed:** 2026-11-06
* **Source filename:** ''iet-scout-electronics-badge-leaders-notes.pdf''
* **Cover title:** //Scout Electronics Badge Leaders’ Pack//
* **Length:** 37 PDF pages

===== Review scope =====

I extracted the searchable text and compared it with the learner booklet and the new [[sites:scouts-electronics-badge:leaders-guide|Leaders’ Guide]]. I did not visually inspect the pages, photographs, or circuit diagrams. Some extracted text is garbled or appears out of order, so diagram-led instructions have not been treated as verified. Image reuse rights were not assessed.

===== Useful material carried into the Leaders’ Guide =====

* A flexible, multi-meeting programme can help Leaders pace component learning, resistor markings, meter practice, circuit projects, soldering, and Scout explanations. A six-meeting example has been added as a planning aid, not as a required sequence.
* Group activities can make component identification and resistor codes more engaging. The guide now includes photo-symbol-description matching, a resistor-colour game, and a smartphone/everyday-electronics discussion as adaptable formats.
* The pack's preparation notes reinforce practical session planning: organise parts by team, check equipment in advance, and plan for shared tools that may create a queue. These points are now in the guide.
* The pack presents projects at different levels and includes optional design challenges. The guide records project names as inspiration, while requiring Leaders to verify any design and parts for the intended activity.

===== Material not adopted =====

The pack includes old supplier references and prices, which were not carried over. Some technical advice is unsuitable as general instruction. In particular, it suggests that a coin cell's limited output can make a resistor-less LED acceptable, describes a 5 V USB LED project without a resistor for a particular LED, and gives an outdated fixed USB-current allowance. These are product- and specification-dependent claims; design each circuit using the actual supply and component data, including suitable current limiting and protection against shorts.

The pack also contains generic soldering advice but does not provide the current, specific fume-control guidance now linked in the Leaders’ Guide. Its mains-tool and circuit-breaker recommendations were not adopted as universal rules. Use current Scouts policy, the activity risk assessment, HSE guidance, and the actual tool instructions.

===== Disposition =====

''Source Documents/iet-scout-electronics-badge-leaders-notes.pdf'' was deleted from the repository after the review, following the established source-review workflow. This note records the review and its limits; it is not a substitute for the source document.
