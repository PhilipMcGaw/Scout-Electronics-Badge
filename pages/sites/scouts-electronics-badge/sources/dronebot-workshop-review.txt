====== DroneBot Workshop source review ======

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-15

**Source:** [[https://dronebotworkshop.com/electronics-index/|DroneBot Workshop electronics index]], with the linked [[https://dronebotworkshop.com/project/|Projects]] and [[https://dronebotworkshop.com/tutorials/|Tutorials]] indexes and selected articles below.

===== Assessment =====

The site is a useful discovery source for practical microcontroller, sensor and robotics topics. Its strongest fit with current project work is the sensor material: calibration, choosing between analogue and digital interfaces, and building a weather data logger. The project and tutorial indexes are broad and frequently updated; use them to find relevant articles rather than treating the indexes as a reviewed curriculum. Articles combine explanations, code, wiring examples and product-specific claims, so check current primary documentation before adapting any circuit or specification. The source is US-based; its units, products, board assumptions and safety context may not transfer directly to the UK. Do not reuse its text, code or images without checking their terms.

===== Related example =====

The Raspberry Pi article [[https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/self-sufficient-mobile-weather-station/|Self-sufficient mobile weather station]] (published 2026-11-09) is a useful project case study rather than a build guide. It describes a portable, solar-powered station measuring temperature, humidity, wind speed and light, with field testing in Wales. Its reported testing also makes good discussion points: bearing friction caused low-speed wind readings to stall, and a warm sensor head read about 1–2 °C above expectation. These are reported results from that project, not general sensor performance claims. The article illustrates why enclosure design, sensor placement, calibration/validation and field testing matter. Its IP65 description is the builder’s account; do not treat it as independent certification or copy the design without checking requirements.

===== Project use =====

* Add calibration, sensor-interface comparison and sensor placement as reading leads for the sensor data-logging draft. Keep the draft’s requirement to verify the selected board, sensor, circuit and storage hardware.
* Use the weather-station article as an optional discussion example for measurement quality and iterative field testing; do not reproduce its design, images or results as project experience.
* The index also points to digital potentiometers, logic, power supplies and motor drivers. These overlap existing project topics, but the indexes alone do not establish that any individual article is suitable or technically current.
* This review is not a UK safety assessment or a validation of any featured circuit, code or product specification.
