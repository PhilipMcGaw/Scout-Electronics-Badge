====== Blog drafts ======

These files are working outlines and first drafts for the blog posts linked to the Scouts electronics booklet. Check technical claims and source permissions before publication, and keep Philip’s reported experience distinct from general guidance.

The order below is a suggested reading sequence, not a publication schedule. The first ten posts build from basic concepts to practical projects; later posts are optional, more specialised topics. Add a link in the **Published article** column when a draft is published. A link to an existing article being revised can be recorded there too; use `—` when there is no matching live article yet.
