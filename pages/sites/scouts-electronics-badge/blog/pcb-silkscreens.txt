====== Reading PCB silkscreens ======

**Status:** Outline started.

**Related booklet section:** [[sites:scouts-electronics-badge:booklet#read//the//board_markings|Read the board markings]]

**Purpose:** Show how silkscreen markings help a builder identify parts, position them, and check their orientation.

===== Planned outline =====

- What the silkscreen is and how it relates to copper pads and the board layout.
- Read reference designators such as ''R1'', ''C1'', ''D1'', and ''U1'' alongside a BOM or schematic.
- Find polarity markings for diodes and LEDs, and confirm them against the component and assembly guide.
- Identify an IC outline, its notch or pin-1 mark, and the package’s pin numbering.
- Explain why markings are helpful but should be checked against the schematic, datasheet, and assembly instructions.
