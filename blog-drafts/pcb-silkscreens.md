# Reading PCB silkscreens

**Status:** Outline started.

**Related booklet section:** [Read the board markings](../Scouts%20Electronics%20Activity%20Badge%20Booklet.md#read-the-board-markings)

**Purpose:** Show how silkscreen markings help a builder identify parts, position them, and check their orientation.

## Planned outline

1. What the silkscreen is and how it relates to copper pads and the board layout.
2. Read reference designators such as `R1`, `C1`, `D1`, and `U1` alongside a BOM or schematic.
3. Find polarity markings for diodes and LEDs, and confirm them against the component and assembly guide.
4. Identify an IC outline, its notch or pin-1 mark, and the package’s pin numbering.
5. Explain why markings are helpful but should be checked against the schematic, datasheet, and assembly instructions.
