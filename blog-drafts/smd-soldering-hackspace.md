## Philip’s reported preferences

- He usually hand-solders 0805 parts, sometimes works with 0604 parts, and finds smaller parts hard work.
- For ICs, 1.27 mm SOIC pitch is about his preferred hand-soldering limit.
- He prefers feeding solder to one pin at a time and does not like drag soldering.
- He considers a flux pen essential for hand-soldering SMDs.
- He has a temperature-controlled hot-air rework station and a small hot plate; his reported 400 °C setpoint is specific to his equipment, not a general recipe.
